Hello everyone, here's the mod's contents.



++ Version 1.0

+ Genes:
- HardToStop (a reduced version of Unstoppable).
- NightOwl (forces the Night Owl trait).
- WarmShiftingBlood (an attempt to represent better temperature regulation, primarily through more variable self-heat production).
- TurbineLung (a representation of a strange respiratory system I came up with).
- DigitigradeGait (an excuse to enable more speed options).
- UnguligradeGait (another excuse to enable more speed options).
- Phoenix (Archite: When someone with this gene dies and enough time passes to prioritize other forms of revival, a peculiar resurrection will form from their organs. The resulting baby has the same germline and passions as the original. The gene does not enable resurrection until adulthood, nor will there be any resurrection after the body's destruction. Natural fertility will be extremely low).
- Bio-electric thighs (grants the ability to paralyze the target. This effect can fail or be reduced by toxic tolerance. This statistic was chosen to provide a minimally logical and compatible limit, primarily for animals).
- Hair colors: Mammoth Blonde Hair, Platinum Pink Hair, Military Blonde Hair, Oceanic Deep Ice Hair, Infernal Hair, Deep Inferno Hair. Skin color: Skin Pink. Ears: Horse Ears.
- Twisted Horns: purely aesthetic, but becomes useful with the World of Naruto mod.

+ Artifact.
- Archotech Gene Integrator (a device that converts xenogenes to germline genes, even if they are Archite).

+ Xenotypes.
- Khorsy.
- Nudis.

+ Retexture.
- This mod implements a texture change to cat ears and floppy ears (it won't be disabled).
